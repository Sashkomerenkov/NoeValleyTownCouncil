Council Charter

About Council

The Noe Valley Town Council is an unofficial neighborhood institution dedicated to civic seriousness, proportional satire, and the preservation of local character under mild but constant pressure.

Our Mission

We exist to represent concerns that are too specific for City Hall but too emotionally charged for casual text threads. Our mission is to formalize local anxieties into dignified procedures.

Our Priorities

Primary priorities include stroller lane diplomacy, parking grievance de-escalation, canine style ethics, and architectural interventions that do not resemble airport lounges.

Governing Philosophy

Our approach combines procedural language with neighborhood memory. We believe every block has a spirit, every line has a queue, and every renovation deserves at least one respectful question.

Committee Structure

  • Committee on Sidewalk Etiquette and Rotational Yield
  • Standing Board for Dog Ownership Aesthetics
  • Architectural Review and Material Restraint Panel
  • Task Force on Parking Sentiment and Curb Fairness
  • Joint Desk for Matcha Queue Continuity

Institutional History

The Council began as a handwritten agenda taped to a cafe corkboard. Within months, regular sessions emerged to discuss curb etiquette, construction fatigue, and alarming levels of confidence in backyard hardscape proposals.

Today, the Council remains proudly unofficial while maintaining an exemplary record of minutes, memos, and emotionally specific neighborhood findings.

Public Participation

Residents may attend open sessions, submit concerns, and request symbolic intervention. Capacity is first come, first seated, and occasionally first to bring pastries.
